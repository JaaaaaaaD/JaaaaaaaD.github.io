Research Experience

Robotic Arm Error Compensation
Research Project · Feb 2025 – Apr 2025

Pelvic Point Cloud Registration for Medical CT Images
Research Project · May 2025 – Jul 2025

Developed a visualization panel using PyQt5 and Open3D for registration of pre-, intra-, and postoperative pelvic CT point clouds. The workflow combined point cloud cleaning, manual keypoint selection, PCA-based alignment, and ICP registration.

Medical CT Image Metal Marker Detection
Research Project · Sep 2025 – Nov 2025

Conducted image processing and marker retrieval on intraoperative CT data using SimpleITK, nibabel, Hough transform, and DBSCAN to identify metal sphere markers and recover their spatial relationships within the image volume.
