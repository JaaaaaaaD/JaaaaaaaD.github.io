Research Direction

I am interested in intelligence as an integrated capability: perception, cognition, action, learning, and adaptation should eventually be understood within one coherent framework. My current work uses computer vision and multimodal learning as concrete entry points toward this broader question.

Instance Segmentation Under Occlusion

Agricultural vision · dataset construction

My work on first-person maize instance segmentation focuses on benchmark construction, annotation protocols, instance ownership, and model evaluation for agricultural vision.

Medical Image Analysis

CT markers · lesion segmentation · 3D geometry

My medical imaging work combines practical detection pipelines, point-cloud visualization, and segmentation-model comparison for CT analysis and lesion segmentation.

Multimodal Visual Grounding

EEG · object regions · long-sequence signals

I am exploring EEG-guided visual localization as a multimodal grounding problem that links cognitive signals with object-level visual regions.

Brain-inspired and Embodied Intelligence

unified intelligence · AR/XR · perception-action systems

I am drawn to intelligence as an integrated system, where perception, memory, grounding, action, and adaptation are not separate endpoints but parts of one coherent mechanism.
